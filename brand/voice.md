# Brand Voice: The AI Agent Factory

The brand is the book *The AI Agent Factory* (Panaversity). It teaches people to work with AI by assigning, governing, and managing an AI Worker. The audience is everyone who uses AI in their daily workflows, from beginners to heavy users. Book intro: https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/five-horizons

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Clear.** Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. No jargon without a plain explanation.
- **Practical.** Say what the reader can do, try, or learn. Prefer a real example to a big idea.
- **Honest.** Say what we know and what is only a forecast. Never oversell.

## 2. Words we use

- **AI Worker** – always capitalised. This is our name for an AI you assign work to and manage.
- **Horizon 1** – the era we are in now, where people assign, govern, and manage an AI Worker.
- **Assign, govern, manage** – the three jobs of a person working with an AI Worker.
- Plain words: "learn", "try", "set up", "check", "decide", "accountable".
- Forecast language for later horizons: "may", "the book argues", "one possible path".

## 3. Words we avoid

- Hype words: revolutionary, game-changer, groundbreaking, unleash, supercharge, mind-blowing, "the future is here".
- Fear or pressure words: "don't get left behind", "replace you", "last chance".
- Needless jargon and buzzwords. If a term is needed, explain it in the same sentence.
- Absolute words about the future: "will definitely", "guaranteed", "always", "never fails".

## 4. Claims we must never make

1. **No income, job, or promotion outcomes.** Never say the book will help someone earn more, get hired, or avoid being replaced.
2. **No guaranteed results.** Never say reading the book makes anyone an expert or saves a set amount of time.
3. **No predictions as fact.** Horizon 2 and later are forecasts. Write "may" or "the book argues", never "will happen".
4. **No claim that AI Workers are reliable or safe without human oversight.** Humans set purpose, define authority, and stay accountable.
5. **No claims about specific AI products or companies.** No "works with X" or "better than Y" unless we can prove it.
6. **No fake urgency or scarcity.** No countdowns, "last chance", or "only a few copies left" unless it is true.
7. **No unverified endorsements, reviews, or reader numbers.** Use only quotes and figures we have checked.

## 5. How we sign off

Close every email with one short line, then the sign-off, then the link.

> Start with Horizon 1. Learn to manage one AI Worker well.
>
> The AI Agent Factory team
>
> Read the introduction: https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/five-horizons

- Sender name: **The AI Agent Factory team** (change this if emails should come from a named person).
- One call to action per email. Keep the link text plain and honest, such as "Read the introduction".
- Do not add a postscript that adds pressure or a new claim.
